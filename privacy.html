dCipher Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Overview

dCipher helps users review conversations and receive AI-powered communication insights. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how users can delete their data.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: alias, phone number, and profile display name
  • Chat content: messages sent in the app
  • AI analysis inputs/outputs: messages selected for analysis and returned insights
  • Technical data: app version/build, request identifiers, and basic diagnostic logs

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To deliver chat functionality
  • To generate conversation/message insights requested by the user
  • To improve reliability, security, and performance

Data Processing and Storage

  • Core app backend data is processed and stored on AWS services (including API, compute, and database services)
  • Some legacy Firebase dependencies may remain in the app binary during transition periods, but active production runtime paths may be AWS-backed depending on app configuration

AI Processing

  • When a user requests analysis, relevant chat/message content may be sent to an AI provider to produce safety/communication insights
  • AI outputs are informational and are not medical, legal, or emergency advice

Data Retention

  • We retain account and chat data while the account remains active
  • Users can delete their account and associated app data in-app via Settings > Delete Account

Your Choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time
  • You may delete your account in-app
  • You may contact support for privacy questions or requests

Children

dCipher is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect user data. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
